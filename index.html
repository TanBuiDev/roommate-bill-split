Privacy overview

Your email sign-in and account data are handled by an external authentication and database service. Resident names and saved bills are stored under your account. The browser may retain limited session and form preferences. Avoid entering unrelated sensitive information.

Terms of use

This tool offers a suggested allocation based on the days each resident stayed. Verify the figures with your household. It does not measure utilities or process financial payments.
